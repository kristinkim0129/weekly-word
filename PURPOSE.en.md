# After Sermon (함께묵상) — Why this app

> After Sermon — Pray the week together.  
> From Sunday Word to daily prayer with your friends.

---

## A note from the founder

We’re people who believe community matters.

There are already many tools for reading Scripture alone.  
We built After Sermon (함께묵상) so the week after the sermon can be lived with friends and church — not in isolation.

We want Sunday’s Word to guide the week, prayer to bind us to one another, and life with God to be fuller together. That’s all we’re after.

---

## The gap we saw

Apps that help people read the Bible already exist.  
They send reminders, offer plans, and track reading streaks.  
More apps now help you **keep the sermon** too.

What is harder to find is a week that **connects friends** and **comes alive inside a community**.

- In a church cell or small group, “What did you hear on Sunday?” often ends inside each person’s own notes app.
- Friends who live far apart want accountability, but group chats fade.
- Personal devotion can go deep while **prayer for each other** and **shared practice** stay disconnected.

After Sermon exists to fill that gap.

---

## Purpose

**After the sermon — live the week together.**

Capture the Word. Check in through daily prayer.  
Stay linked to a small circle of **accountability buddies** — friends and church — so the journey is not isolated.

We are not trying to make people “read more.”  
We want **Sunday’s Word to guide the week**, lived more fully to God, with your people.

---

## Who it’s for

| Setting | People |
| --- | --- |
| Church community | Cells, small groups, young adults |
| Friends at a distance | Friends who want to live faith together even when apart |
| Small bonds | Even two or a few people walking one season together |

We prioritize names you can picture — not a platform of anonymous followers.

---

## Principles we hold

### 1. Protect the personal; share by choice

Deep thoughts and private notes stay **with me**.  
Prayer requests, meditation points, and this week’s practice can be **with the group**.

### 2. Reading alone is not the end

Capture the Word → check in after prayer each day →  
pray for one another. That is one flow.

### 3. Companionship has a season

Walk together with a defined period — H1, H2, a year, a short stretch, or custom.

### 4. Technology does not replace worship

The app does not replace Scripture or community.  
It helps people **not forget**, **not drift apart**, and **look toward one another again**.

---

## What “success” looks like

- A cell leader asks, “Did you capture this week’s Word?”
- A friend far away leaves “I prayed” thinking of you.
- At the end of a season: “We walked this together.”
- Meditation becomes **care for one another**.

it helps Sunday’s Word guide the week, through daily prayer with friends and church.**
