## Why it exists

Most apps help you *read more*.  
Sundaily helps you **stay with one Word** — and stay with your people.

Capture what landed. Return to it. Pray. Check in.  
Send *I prayed.* Walk a season. Look back.

No heavy plans. No streaks that shame you.  
Just a quiet place for Sunday to keep speaking.

## The week in four beats

**Capture → Meditate → Apply → Reflect**

- **Sunday** — Write the verse or message that landed.  
- **Mon–Tue** — Reread. Pray. Notice your day.  
- **Wed–Thu** — One small obedience.  
- **Fri–Sat** — Look back gently. Prepare for next Sunday.

---

## Who it’s for

Cell groups · close friends · people far from home who still want to walk the week in faith.

Face-to-face names — not a feed of strangers.

---

## Product truths

1. Deep notes stay private. Prayer & practice can be shared.  
2. Tech doesn’t replace church — it helps you remember one another.  
3. Seasons end on purpose, so you can say: *we walked this together.*
